    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need a learner's licence before driving lessons?
    Yes. South African law requires a valid learner's licence for the vehicle code before you drive on public roads. If you don't have one yet, our Learner's Licence Prep lessons help you study road signs, rules and vehicle controls for the test.
    How many lessons does a beginner typically need?
    Every learner is different, but complete beginners often need around 15–20 lessons to be test-ready, while drivers with some practice may need 8–12. Your baseline drive gives you an honest recommendation.
    What's the difference between Code 8 and Code 10?
    Code 8 (now often shown as code B) covers light motor vehicles such as ordinary cars and light delivery vehicles. Code 10 (code C1) covers heavier vehicles such as minibuses and trucks between 3,500 kg and 16,000 kg. Code 10 holders may also drive Code 8 vehicles.
    Can I use your car for my driving test?
    Yes. Test-day car hire includes the use of a familiar, roadworthy school vehicle and a short warm-up drive before your test. Book it with your package or as an add-on.
    Which areas do you pick up from?
    We're based in Florida, Roodepoort, and offer pickups across Roodepoort and nearby West Rand suburbs. Tell us your suburb in the booking form and we'll confirm availability.
